Personal AI assistant

Saul, on your phone.

A native Android client for your conversations, agent activity, and saved context. Your assistant runs on its own backend, not on this download site.

Download Android APK

Android 7.0 or newer. Direct installation, outside Google Play. The download becomes available after the first signed release is published.

Install and connect

  1. Download the APK and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager.
  2. Open Saul and choose Sign in with Web.
  3. Sign in to the backend account, approve access, and return to Saul.

The APK connects to the server selected for that release. You need an account on that server; downloading does not create one. Ask its administrator for access.
